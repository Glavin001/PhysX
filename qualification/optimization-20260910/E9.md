# E9 — cooperative off-diagonal rows

Rejected and reverted. Four lanes evaluate consecutive edges, with lane zero
adding in original CSR order. This exposes independent work without changing
precision, operator or required convergence. Three numerical tests, 3D memcheck,
initcheck and synccheck, plus direct polynomial subgroup synccheck pass. Wall
invariants pass and all four A wall outputs are byte-identical; historical
golden remains unresolved. Component resources:111 registers,48-byte stack,
1,600 shared bytes.
