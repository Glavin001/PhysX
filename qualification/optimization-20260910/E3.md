# E3 — reuse unchanged fine factors

**Verified relative application gain.** Three matched 600-step repeats per arm
and scenario reduce heavy-destruction mean by 2.697 ms (4.05%) against the closer
A control. No repeatable idle regression or established peak/deadline improvement.
Preserved independently while later experiments run; installed SDK remains A.

| Scenario | A-before mean ms | E3 mean ms | A-after mean ms | A worst peak ms | E3 worst peak ms |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Idle | 1.566240 | 1.666709 | 1.593809 | 17.735776 | 17.288351 |
| Heavy destruction | 66.696298 | 63.841311 | 66.538422 | 246.486877 | 235.152676 |

Every heavy repeat exceeds 8 ms, 1000/120 ms and 1000/60 ms on 519/600 steps
(86.5%). Idle exceeds 8 ms/120 Hz on 1/600 steps per repeat (0.167%); 60 Hz on
0–1/600. All startup steps are retained. Mean initialization plus 600 steps:
heavy 42,515.429 / 40,623.776 / 42,345.635 ms; idle
3,356.020 / 3,634.203 / 3,273.340 ms, in A-before/E3/A-after order.

The apparent idle regression prompted five more 600-step repeats per arm:
1.595094 / 1.617094 / 1.627638 ms, with overlapping individual means.
Initialization plus steps: 3,336.762 / 3,287.706 / 3,395.100 ms. The regression
was not reproduced consistently; no idle or startup speedup is claimed.
The earlier two-repeat 180-step screen showed ~5.1% lower heavy mean. Its
shorter duration is not mixed with the 600-step result.

The existing inverse-lifetime producer certifies unchanged incident live bonds
before advancing each node's generation. A valid inverse was built from a
validated fine factor, so E3 reuses that factor under the same generation proof.
Changed rows, first use and stale/unknown proofs retain original assembly and
checks. Geometry, coefficients, numerical tolerances and physics remain fixed.

Three numerical tests, direct cold/cut/stale/future/unknown factor comparisons,
factor memcheck/initcheck and 3D memcheck/initcheck/synccheck pass. Wall invariants
pass; poses, decompressed motion, launch tape and body words match A byte for
byte. All four 600-step physical-history comparisons match. Historical golden,
migration and baseline correction-memory findings remain unresolved, not waived.
