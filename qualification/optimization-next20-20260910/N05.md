# N05 — component-sized motion-tour construction — rejected

Hypothesis: an Euler tour stays within one unknown component, so pointer jumping needs a bound of twice its node count minus two, rather than twice every bond in the city. The initial largest component has380 unknowns; unrelated buildings cannot lengthen its tour. Existing E12 Systems evidence attributes304.244 ms across157 construction launches, or1.69 ms per measured heavy step. Estimated application saving0.3–0.9 ms is a hypothesis, not a measured gain.

One coherent change: reduce the maximum component tour length on device and use it for the existing pointer-jump loop. Keep connectivity, exact geometric closure, factor and original-equation checks unchanged. Idle initialization, fracture correction and later topology changes are affected; retained setup generations are not rebuilt.

Attempt1, commit `83f6543e4b28f76ce95d3ee84ca2111d0c258e62`, failed the analytic split-component test. Its new reuse of the shared control word could race readers of the preceding uniform branch. Attempt2 adds a cooperative barrier before reusing that word, commit `7588a85e81b3202ff0ea23dbfa5da02f4bae7d07`. The corrected candidate passes all three existing numerical/motion tests,3D memcheck/initcheck/synccheck, and motion-mode memcheck/synccheck. The600-step wall invariant subset passes and its four recorded physical outputs are byte-identical to the preserved control. The old historical topology golden still fails; it is not waived.

Heavy mean is only0.0924 ms below the closer E12 control (0.134%); idle mean is above both controls and peak timing does not improve. No verified suite win. Revert.
